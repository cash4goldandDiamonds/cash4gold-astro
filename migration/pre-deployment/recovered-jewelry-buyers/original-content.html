What’s the difference between scrap gold and jewelry gold?

Scrap gold is loose metal, bits, or damaged items bought by weight and purity. Jewelry gold includes intact pieces (rings, necklaces, bracelets) that may have design, brand, or collector value above the melt price. Buyers like Cash 4 Gold & Diamonds evaluate both, but intact, branded pieces often fetch more because they can be resold or restored rather than melted down.
