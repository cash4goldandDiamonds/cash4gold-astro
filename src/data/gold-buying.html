A DIRECT LOCAL PURCHASE

Understand your offer before you decide

A gold jewelry purchase is based on the item in front of us. Metal content and weight matter; diamonds, construction, condition, and other characteristics may also need separate consideration.

For background on markings and purity, see the World Gold Council’s guide to gold jewelry. You can also read our explanation of how gold is valued before your visit.

  1. Bring your gold or coins

    Bring the items you want to sell, plus any receipts, grading reports, or packaging you already have. Let us know if the collection is large.

  2. Review the evaluation

    Ask how the offer accounts for the gold and any diamonds. Review the final purchase amount before agreeing to sell.

  3. Choose your payment method

    Payment is available by cash, bank wire, or business check. Discuss your preferred method when arranging a substantial transaction.

Questions about selling gold in Downtown LA

Do you buy broken gold jewelry and scrap gold?

Yes. We buy broken chains, damaged gold jewelry, single earrings, and mixed scrap gold. Bring the actual items for an evaluation.

Can I sell a Cuban chain with diamonds?

Yes. We buy Cuban chains and bracelets with or without diamonds. Ask how both the gold and any diamonds are considered in the offer.

Do you buy large quantities of gold and silver coins?

Yes. We welcome gold and silver coins in quantity. For a substantial collection, call 310-663-1340 to arrange time to review the items.

How can I receive payment?

Payment is available by cash, bank wire, or business check. Discuss the method you prefer before completing the transaction.

Where is your gold-buying location?

Cash 4 Gold & Diamonds is at 617 S. Hill Street, Los Angeles, CA 90014, in the Downtown Los Angeles Jewelry District. Contact us to arrange your visit.

Will a gold-price calculator give me the final offer?

A calculator is an estimate, not a purchase offer. The final amount depends on evaluation of your actual items and the agreed terms of sale. Read how jewelry buyers evaluate gold for more context.
