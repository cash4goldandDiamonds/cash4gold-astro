Website Privacy Information

This information describes the Cash 4 Gold & Diamonds website preview and its contact and privacy choices.

Current preview: online inquiry delivery and optional Google Analytics are disabled. You can contact the business by phone or email. The sections below explain the prepared features before they are enabled.

Browsing and website security

The preview uses Cloudflare to serve and protect its pages. Authorized preview access may require a Cloudflare sign-in. This website does not provide customer account registration, a newsletter signup or an on-site payment form.

Cloudflare processes technical request information, which can include an IP address and browser information, to provide hosting and security. When the online inquiry feature is available, Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions for automated abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy information.

Contact details and online inquiries

You choose what to share when calling, emailing or asking about your jewelry. An available online inquiry form asks for a name, email address, item type and message, with an optional phone number. Its permission checkbox allows the business to use those details to respond to that inquiry.

The prepared form uses Resend to forward accepted inquiries to the business mailbox. Messages include a reference number so the business can identify the inquiry. An email-service acceptance does not confirm inbox receipt or reserve an appointment. See Resend's privacy information.

Do not include identification documents, payment-card details, banking information or other sensitive material in an inquiry. The form does not accept file uploads. Ask the business how to provide any documents needed for a particular transaction.

Optional analytics and your choice

If optional Google Analytics is enabled, you can accept or reject it in Privacy preferences. The website does not load its optional Google Analytics script until you affirmatively allow analytics. Calling, emailing, following an appointment link and sending an available inquiry do not require analytics consent.

When allowed, analytics helps the business understand page visits and contact actions. The website's custom contact-action events record the action type and site page rather than the name, email, phone number or message entered in the inquiry form. Google may also process browser, device and cookie information as described in Google's privacy policy. Advertising storage and personalized advertising are disabled in this version.

Open Privacy preferences to change or withdraw your choice. Your saved analytics choice is valid for up to 180 days; after that, a site with analytics enabled asks again. This validity period does not automatically erase browser storage or describe how long the business or a service provider retains other records. You can remove this site's saved browser data using your browser settings.

Menu sound preference

The menu can remember whether you choose sounds on or off using storage in your browser. This setting does not require your name or contact details. Change it with the menu sound control or clear this site's saved browser data.

Appointment links and other websites

Phone and email links open the calling or email service on your device. Appointment links can open Calendly, where you choose the information to provide. Maps, social profiles and research links also take you to other services with their own privacy practices. See Calendly's privacy notice before using its scheduling service.
