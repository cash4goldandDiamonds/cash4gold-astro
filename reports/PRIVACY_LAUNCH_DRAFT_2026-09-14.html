Website Privacy Information

This notice explains information processed through the Cash 4 Gold & Diamonds website when you browse, contact us or use available website features.

Browsing and website security

Cloudflare helps deliver and protect this website. It processes technical request information, which can include your IP address and browser information, to provide hosting and security. If an online inquiry form is available, Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated abuse before a message can be sent. See Cloudflare's privacy information.

This website does not offer customer account registration, a newsletter signup or an on-site payment form.

Contacting the business

You choose what to share when you call or email us. When an online inquiry form is available, it asks for your name, email address, item type and message. A phone number is optional. The form asks permission to use those details to respond to your inquiry; that permission is not a signup for marketing.

When available, the online form uses Resend to forward accepted inquiries to the business mailbox. The message includes the contact details and description you provide, along with a reference number. See Resend's privacy information. A message accepted by the email service is not a guarantee of inbox receipt or an appointment confirmation.

Do not include identification documents, banking information, payment-card details or other sensitive material in a website inquiry. The form does not accept file uploads. Ask the business how to provide any documents needed for a particular transaction.

Optional analytics

If Google Analytics is enabled on this website, Privacy preferences lets you choose whether to allow it. The website does not load its optional Google Analytics script before you allow analytics. Calling, emailing, opening an appointment link and sending an available inquiry do not require analytics consent.

When you allow analytics, it helps the business understand page visits and contact actions. The website's own contact-action events include the action and the site page, without the name, email address, phone number or message entered in the inquiry form. Google can also process browser, device and cookie information. See Google's privacy policy.

These website analytics controls keep advertising storage and personalized advertising off. Opening an appointment link is measured as a click, not a confirmed booking; a form acceptance is not measured as confirmed inbox delivery.

Changing your browser preferences

Open Privacy preferences to change or withdraw your analytics choice. Rejecting optional analytics keeps it off. Withdrawing an earlier permission disables optional analytics on the current page and clears the accessible analytics cookies used by this setup. When the saved choice can be updated or removed, the page reloads to stop the loaded analytics script. If browser storage prevents both, analytics stays off on the current page and a notice explains that the choice applies only to that page. You can remove the earlier saved choice in your browser settings.

Your saved analytics choice is valid for up to 180 days. After it expires, analytics stays off until you choose to allow it again. If browser storage is unavailable, the page explains that your choice applies only to that page. You can also remove this site's saved browser data using your browser settings.

The menu can separately remember whether you choose sounds on or off. This preference does not require your name or contact details. Change it using the menu sound control or clear this site's saved browser data.

How long information is kept

We keep customer inquiries only as long as needed to answer questions and complete transactions, and we delete them upon request. All privacy and deletion requests are handled by the owner.

The 180-day analytics preference period describes how long a saved permission remains valid. It does not describe how long inquiry messages, business records or service-provider data are retained, and it does not automatically delete the saved browser record.

Appointment links and other services

Phone and email links open your device's calling or email service. Appointment links can open Calendly, where you choose the information to provide. Review Calendly's privacy notice before using its scheduling service.

Maps, social profiles and other external links take you to services with their own privacy practices. The contact choices on this website do not change the privacy settings of those services.
